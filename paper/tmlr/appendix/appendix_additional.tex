
\section{Prompt Contract and Target Examples}
\label{app:targets}

The logical prompt content is fixed across conditions except for the format specification.

\begin{center}
    \setlength{\fboxsep}{10pt}
    \colorbox{oaSoft}{%
        \parbox{0.94\linewidth}{%
            \small
            \textbf{Prompt template}\par\smallskip
            {\ttfamily
            Use only the provided context. Context sentences are labeled [S1], [S2], \dots\par
            Do not use outside knowledge.\par
            Required output format: \emph{FORMAT\_SPEC}\par\medskip
            Question:\par
            \emph{QUESTION}\par\medskip
            Context:\par
            \emph{LABELED\_CONTEXT}\par\medskip
            Response:}
        }%
    }
\end{center}

For a toy example with answer $a=\text{24 months}$, support sentence ID $s=2$, and support sentence \texttt{The device includes a 24-month warranty.}, the compiled targets are:

\begin{table}[htbp]
    \centering
    \small
    \setlength{\tabcolsep}{5pt}
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.08}
    \begin{tabularx}{0.96\linewidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash\bfseries}p{1.05cm} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Cond.} & \textbf{Compiled target} \\
        \midrule
        Plain & {\ttfamily 24 months} \\
        \overlay{J} & {\ttfamily \{"answer":"24 months"\}} \\
        \overlay{Q} & {\ttfamily ANSWER: 24 months\par QUOTE: "The device includes a 24-month warranty."} \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

For strict metrics, malformed outputs are scored as failures rather than repaired by auxiliary heuristics. This strictness is intentional: it makes the paper's recovery claim more falsifiable and the evaluation trail easier to audit.
